# The board: a fallback

Use it only when the user won't go look and would rather pick from options. Real references they choose themselves are always better; say so once.

- Offer five references labeled A to E, one or two lines each: a real work and the one attribute you would take from it.
- Make them far from each other: different eras, places, media and registers (crafts, science, sport, music, cinema, nature, everyday objects). At least two from outside the requested medium.
- Prefer the user's culture and region when you know it.
- Avoid the references everyone already uses (Wes Anderson symmetry, Blade Runner neon, Studio Ghibli, Apple minimalism, Bauhaus posters) unless the user asks: they are the default of references.
- Only real works you are sure exist. If unsure, describe the kind of object instead of inventing a title, an author or a link.
- They pick up to three letters and say what they like; "dale" means you pick two that fit plus one wildcard and say which. Then go to step 4.
