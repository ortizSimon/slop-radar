# The usual defaults, by medium

What an assistant gives first when nobody gives it a reference. Use it to make the reaction concrete, not as a list to dodge: the way out is the user's references, not the opposite of this list.

- **Metaphors and lines:** time as a river, sand slipping through fingers, a weaver; life as a journey; love as fire.
- **Business names:** "Noun & Noun" (Grano & Luna), "La [Noun] [Adjective]", Marea, Aroma, Esencia, Alma, Raíz; English words for a Spanish-speaking business; product puns.
- **Slogans and taglines:** "Más que un X", "Tu mejor versión", "Where X meets Y", three words with periods ("Fuerza. Pasión. Resultados.").
- **Captions, bios and hooks:** "En un mundo donde…", a rhetorical question, sparkle emojis, a block of hashtags, "¿Sabías que…?", "3 tips para…", "Arte en tu piel".
- **Stories and characters:** a watchmaker, a restorer of old books, a lighthouse keeper; names like Elías or Elias Thorne, Aurelio, Elena; a coastal town, rain, a mysterious letter.
- **Speeches and cards:** "Desde el día que te conocí…", definitions of love or friendship, a famous quote.
- **Logos:** the product as a symbol (wheat for a bakery, a steaming cup for a café, a microphone with waves for a podcast, a leaf, a lightbulb) inside a circle, "Est. 19XX", brown and gold or a blue-violet gradient.
- **Image prompts:** "cinematic, 8k, golden hour, bokeh", the subject centered and symmetric, teal and orange, glossy skin.
- **Posters:** a saxophone silhouette in gold on navy, notes floating, art deco type, smoke.
- **Landing pages:** Inter or Space Grotesk, a purple gradient on white or near-black with one acid accent, a centered headline with two buttons, a floating phone mockup, three icon cards, a stats row, every section fading up, "Revolutionize your X", "Tomá el control de tus finanzas".
- **App screens:** a big ring or wave of progress in the center, a gradient background, a streak with a flame, "¡Seguí así!" with an emoji.
- **Reels and video:** "¿Sabías que…?", stock b-roll under trending audio, a text listicle, slow pans over products, "Seguime para más".
- **Decks:** a title slide with a gradient, three icons in a row, "Agenda", "Gracias".
- **Advice, plans and strategy:** five to seven generic tips ("definí tu público objetivo", "usá redes sociales", "ofrecé un valor diferencial", "medí tus resultados"), buzzwords, "depende de tu caso" without asking about the case, symmetrical lists with no numbers, and a plan that would work the same for any business.
